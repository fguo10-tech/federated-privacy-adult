# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We, as contributors and maintainers, want taking part in this project to be free of harassment
for everyone, whatever their age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic
status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation. We commit to acting and interacting in ways that keep this community open,
welcoming, diverse, inclusive and healthy.

## Expected behaviour

- Show empathy and kindness toward other people.
- Respect differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences, including disagreement about methods
  and results.
- Give constructive feedback and accept it gracefully.
- Take responsibility for mistakes, apologise to those affected, and learn from them.
- Report results honestly, including results that do not support your hypothesis or ours.
- Put the good of the community ahead of individual advantage.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing other people's private information, such as a physical or e-mail address, without
  their explicit permission.
- Pressuring someone to change or withhold a result, or misrepresenting someone else's work as
  an endorsement.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces: the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions and
reviews. It also applies when someone represents the project in public, for example by posting
from an official account or acting as a representative at an event.

## Reporting

Report incidents privately to the maintainers through the private channel described in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Do not report conduct issues in a public issue. Maintainers will
review every report and respect the privacy and safety of the person reporting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may remove, edit
or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that do not follow this code,
and will explain moderation decisions when appropriate. They decide on consequences using these
steps:

1. **Correction.** For inappropriate language or other behaviour judged unprofessional or
   unwelcome: a private written warning that explains the problem. A public apology may be
   requested.
2. **Warning.** For a single serious incident or a series of actions: a warning with
   consequences for continued behaviour, and no interaction with the people involved for a
   stated period.
3. **Temporary ban.** For a serious violation, including sustained inappropriate behaviour: a
   temporary ban from any interaction or public communication with the community.
4. **Permanent ban.** For a pattern of violations, harassment of an individual, or aggression
   toward or disparagement of groups of people: a permanent ban from public interaction within
   the community.
